# **App Name**: RC SourceoftheNile

## Core Features:

- Homepage Showcase: Curated display of club activities, mission statement, and calls to action for immediate engagement.
- Historical Archive: Chronological compendium of past presidents with bios and achievements, preserving club legacy.
- Project Directory: Comprehensive listing of club projects with detailed descriptions, images, and impact summaries.
- Fellowship Updates: Weekly summaries of fellowship gatherings with text highlights and photo uploads to keep members informed.
- Events Calendar: Interactive schedule of upcoming club events, meetings, and community service opportunities with event details and summaries.
- Dynamic Resource Links: Categorized directory of essential Rotary International and local resources with admin-managed link updates.
- Impactful Photo Selection Tool: AI tool that reviews uploaded photos from members and decides which of them will be best suited for website display. This helps to select only high quality photos for display. The tool also incorporates a bias towards images of local people who are beneficiaries of club projects.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep, saturated blue (#3F51B5), inspired by the 'Source of the Nile', suggesting stability, trust, and depth. This vivid color aligns with the serious work that the organization is accomplishing, while the saturation creates the visual interest that the user is looking for on the web.
- Background color: Light blue (#E8EAF6), a desaturated variant of the primary hue, offering a calm and unobtrusive backdrop.
- Accent color: Warm orange (#FF9800), analogous to blue and positioned to the "left" in the color wheel, adds a vibrant contrast for calls to action and highlights.
- Font pairing: 'Playfair' (serif) for headlines to convey elegance and tradition, paired with 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for body text to ensure readability.
- Use of clean, modern icons to represent different sections and actions, enhancing user navigation and visual appeal.
- A clear, grid-based layout promoting easy navigation. Mobile-first design ensures responsiveness across devices.
- Subtle animations using Framer Motion to create smooth transitions and interactive elements, adding a modern and engaging feel.
